About Lime Pointing

Lime pointing is the traditional craft of renewing mortar joints in brick and stone walls using breathable lime-based mortar. This heritage technique preserves the structural integrity of historic buildings while respecting their original construction methods.

Heritage Restoration

Authentic lime mortar techniques for period properties built before 1919

Breathable Solutions

Lime mortar allows walls to breathe, preventing moisture damage and damp issues

Flexible & Self-Healing

Accommodates building movement and naturally repairs hairline cracks over time
